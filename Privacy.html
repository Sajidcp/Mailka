Mailka Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Mailka is an Android app (package name com.mailka.app) that lets you send an email to a list of people from your own Gmail account. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does with it.

Information Mailka uses

  • Your Google account email address. When you sign in with Google, Mailka reads your email address to show which account is connected and to send you a test email.
  • Recipient lists. The email addresses and names you paste, import or type into the app.
  • Your message. The subject, the message text and any files you attach.
  • Sending records. Whether each email was marked Sent or Failed, and the times of recent sends, so the app can count how many you sent in the last 24 hours.
  • App password (only if you choose that option). If you connect with a Gmail app password instead of Google sign-in, the app stores your Gmail address and that password in the app’s private storage on your phone.

Google permissions

If you sign in with Google, Mailka requests two permissions:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send), used only to send the emails you write, to the recipients you choose, from your own account.
  • See your email address (userinfo.email), used only to show your connected account.

Mailka cannot read, search, download or delete any email in your Gmail account.

How information is used

Your information is used only to run the features you ask for: connecting your account, sending your emails, showing the status of each email and keeping your list so you can continue later. Mailka does not use it for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Where information is stored

Mailka has no server of its own. Your recipient list, message, sending records and, if used, your app password are stored only on your phone. Attachments are read when you add them and sent with your emails; Mailka does not upload them anywhere else. The sign-in access token is kept in the app’s memory while the app is in use.

Sharing

Mailka does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Your emails are delivered by Google’s Gmail service, because that is how email sending works, and Google’s own privacy policy applies to that service.

Mailka’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect: Tap Disconnect in the app to sign out or remove the saved app password.
  • Remove Google access: Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Mailka and remove access.
  • Delete your data: Tap Clear list in the app, clear the app’s storage in your phone settings, or uninstall Mailka. This removes everything the app stored.

Responsible use

You are responsible for the emails you send and for having the right to contact the people on your list. Please send email only to people who know you.

Children

Mailka is not intended for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
