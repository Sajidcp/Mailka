Mailka

Send one email to many people from your own Gmail, straight from your Android phone.

500 mail in a click

What Mailka does

  • Paste or import a list of email addresses. Mailka finds every address and picks up names.
  • Write one subject and message, add attachments, and send a test to yourself first.
  • Each person gets their own email, starting with “Hi” and their name.
  • You can see which emails were sent and which failed, and retry only the failed ones.

How Mailka uses your Google account

When you sign in with Google, Mailka asks only for permission to send email on your behalf and to see your email address. It cannot read, search or delete your mail.

Your recipient list, message and sent marks are stored only on your phone. Mailka does not sell or share your data, and it has no server of its own.

You can remove Mailka’s access at any time in your Google Account under Security, in the section for third-party apps with account access.
